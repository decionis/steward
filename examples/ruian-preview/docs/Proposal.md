## Make every visit count

Help visitors find a suitable choice, give customers a reason to return, and help merchants deliver useful content and better service with less repetitive work.

We propose a 12-week pilot for two participating merchants at one Ruian location. Decionis Steward would provide the staff workspace around a new customer assistance, engagement and content workflow. The pilot would address all four cooperation directions through a shared merchant knowledge base and measurable customer journeys.

A separate, working Steward preview now demonstrates all four directions with a live AI agent and fictional merchant information. Real data connections, customer consent, delivery channels, staff routing and pilot measurement remain proposed implementation work.

## Why Steward fits this opportunity

The brief centers on customer relationships and service experiences. Steward is the stronger foundation because its current product organizes customer context, surfaces support and growth opportunities, and lets staff review actions with the evidence behind them.

Commerce Gate is better suited to evaluating commercial changes such as discounts, prices and refunds. It can be considered later where a supported integration needs those controls. The pilot does not depend on adding it.

### Existing foundation

- Staff dashboard, account context, evidence and source-health views.
- Opportunity queue with approve, hold and reject review controls.
- Support planning by region and segment, plus a decision timeline.
- Validated server-side contracts and a signal-connector framework.

### Proposed Ruian delivery

- A customer-facing web/QR assistant grounded in approved merchant information.
- Permission-based follow-up, membership prompts and event/community invitations.
- A brand-guided content workflow with merchant approval and export.
- Staff handoff, retail data adapters, measurement and merchant-specific configuration.

Steward currently targets regulated customer operations. Its core demo uses fixtures and does not have ready retail source adapters. The new preview is a separate sample-data application, with no connection to live Steward tenants. Retail integrations and production customer workflows still require delivery and testing. Policy decisions and action execution belong in Decionis or an agreed authorized service.

## Direction 01: Personalized consumption decision support

**Customer moment:** A visitor has limited time and a specific need but cannot easily compare the options.

**Proposed experience:** A QR or mobile-web conversation asks about the visitor's stated preferences, time and budget, then offers a short, explained selection of products, services or experiences from the participating merchants' approved catalogues. Each suggestion links to the relevant merchant or booking destination. A guest can use the assistant without creating a profile.

**Pilot deliverables:** One shared assistant surface; one approved catalogue and FAQ set per merchant; preference prompts; source-linked recommendations; basic event tracking; a staff handoff when information is missing. Price and availability are shown only when the source is current, with a visible check time where relevant.

**Measure:** Qualified next-step rate and time to a useful choice. Bookings or purchases are counted only when linked records are available; recommendation clicks are reported separately.

## Direction 02: User interaction and repeat purchase

**Customer moment:** A useful first visit ends without a relevant reason to return.

**Proposed experience:** Invite a customer to opt in to a merchant's existing membership or contact channel. Staff can review a helpful post-visit message, a relevant return invitation or an invitation to a merchant event/community. Messages use stated interests and permitted purchase history. Membership terms and benefits remain merchant-owned.

**Pilot deliverables:** Two lifecycle journeys per merchant: post-visit support and a return/event invitation. Include consent capture, frequency limits, opt-out handling, staff approval and export to one agreed merchant channel. A new loyalty points engine and a new community platform are outside the pilot.

**Measure:** Thirty-day repeat purchase among observable, opted-in purchasers; opt-in rate; opt-out and complaint rates. A repeat visit or click is not treated as a repeat purchase.

## Direction 03: Brand content production efficiency

**Merchant moment:** A team needs timely content but lacks the capacity to plan, write and resize it consistently.

**Proposed experience:** Turn an approved merchant brief into a coordinated content pack: a weekly plan, copy options, a branded graphic draft and a short-video script/storyboard. Reuse the merchant's voice, approved product facts, assets and offer terms. Staff review and export before publication.

**Pilot deliverables:** One brand kit per merchant; one weekly content pack during the live pilot; two agreed graphic aspect ratios; one short-video script template; copy variants for two selected channels. The preview uses AI-written copy in fixed graphic templates; a pilot can add approved image services and licensed merchant assets within the agreed scope. The pilot includes scripts, not filmed or edited video production.

**Measure:** Median staff minutes per approved content pack and first-pass brand acceptance. Publication and engagement can be tracked separately where channel data is available. Automatic publishing is outside the pilot.

## Direction 04: In-store experience and service upgrade

**Customer moment:** Waiting or uncertainty becomes a missed service opportunity.

**Proposed experience:** Place the same assistant at one agreed service touchpoint per merchant. Visitors can explore relevant options while waiting, ask practical questions, see merchant-supplied directions and request a staff handoff. Staff receive a concise summary so the visitor does not have to restart the conversation.

**Pilot deliverables:** QR entry points for two merchants; a consultation/FAQ flow; approved location guidance; staff escalation; feedback prompts; a simple staff view of unresolved requests. Existing wait-time information is shown only if a verified source is available. The pilot does not replace a queue, booking or POS system.

**Measure:** Median time to a useful answer or accepted staff handoff, unresolved-request rate and a one-question satisfaction score. A handoff is not recorded as a resolved enquiry.

## One connected customer journey

1. **Discover:** A guest scans a QR code and states a need.
2. **Choose:** The assistant offers a short, explained selection using approved merchant facts.
3. **Get help:** A staff member receives the question and context when human help is needed.
4. **Stay connected:** The customer chooses whether to join a merchant's existing channel; follow-up remains permission-based.
5. **Learn:** Approved, aggregated journey events and observed outcomes inform the next content pack and service improvement.

### Proposed delivery architecture

Merchant catalogues, FAQs, brand assets and consented transaction/member exports feed a new merchant knowledge and event service. A separate assistant/content service uses approved model providers. The customer web surface and an adapted Steward staff console consume these services. Actions needing policy evaluation or execution are forwarded to Decionis or an agreed authorized backend.

Steward's current connector framework is a starting point, not a ready retail integration. The pilot includes two agreed data-feed types: catalogue/service information and consented member/transaction events, using secure exports first where appropriate. Direct vendor integrations are scoped after access and field quality are confirmed. New customer data and retention rules require an explicit design; raw signals are not added to Steward's existing record store by assumption.

### Operating controls

- Guests can seek advice without an account. Remembered preferences and follow-up require a clear opt-in.
- Each merchant's identifiable customer data stays separate; Ruian sees agreed aggregate results. Cross-merchant recommendations can use public catalogues without joining private customer records.
- Merchants approve content and outbound campaigns. The pilot introduces no automatic price changes, refunds or publication.
- Stale or missing product facts route to staff. Disable the assistant or a journey when quality or data access fails.
- Agree hosting region, model provider, retention/deletion, permissions and channel terms before live customer use.

## A scoped twelve-week pilot

**Planning scope:** One Ruian location; two merchants; one agreed pilot language; one customer web surface; one adapted staff console; two data-feed types; one existing follow-up channel per merchant. No new native mobile app is included. Delivery timing assumes agreed access, a staffed delivery team and timely merchant approvals.

| Phase                 | Timing     | Deliverable and exit condition                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discover and baseline | Weeks 1-2  | Confirm customer journeys, source quality, brands, channel permissions and measurement. Agree the scope, budget, acceptance criteria and a comparison plan. |
| Build and configure   | Weeks 3-6  | Adapt the preview, implement real staff review/handoff and connect two agreed feed types. Complete merchant content review and data/consent checks.         |
| Controlled launch     | Weeks 7-8  | Train staff, launch at the two touchpoints, inspect answer quality and observe real customer use. Expand exposure only after acceptance checks pass.        |
| Measure and decide    | Weeks 9-12 | Improve the journeys, compare outcomes and costs, and deliver the playbook and pilot report. Decide whether to stop, revise or expand.                      |

Only purchaser cohorts with a full 30-day observation window enter the repeat-purchase result. The week-12 report covers mature cohorts; later cohorts receive a scheduled 30-day follow-up report in the agreed handover.

If source access, a usable catalogue, consent records or a staffed service handoff is unavailable at the week-2 gate, narrow or reschedule the pilot before building the dependent workflow.

## Measure what changes

Agree numerical targets after the baseline, before customer exposure. These are proposed measurement definitions, not claimed performance results.

| Direction          | Primary measure                                                                              | Interpretation                                                                            |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01 Choice          | Sessions completing an agreed next step / eligible assistance sessions                       | Define the next step in advance. Separate clicks, bookings and verified purchases.        |
| 02 Repeat purchase | Observable customers buying again within 30 days / observable first purchasers in the cohort | Count only mature cohorts and disclosed coverage. Compare like-for-like opted-in groups.  |
| 03 Content         | Median staff minutes from brief to approved pack                                             | Include editing and rework. Track approval quality alongside time saved.                  |
| 04 Service         | Median time from enquiry to useful answer or accepted staff handoff                          | Report resolution, handoff and unresolved rates separately. Pair speed with satisfaction. |

Use a two-week baseline and, where traffic permits, a comparable holdout or staggered rollout. Report sample sizes, seasonality, merchant mix and attribution gaps. Use aggregated reporting and permitted pseudonymous event links. If purchase linkage is unavailable, report service and engagement outcomes without claiming sales or repeat-purchase uplift.

Weekly reporting also covers answer accuracy against approved facts, opt-outs/complaints, staff adoption, model/hosting usage and cost per assisted session or approved pack. Serious quality or consent failures pause the affected journey pending merchant review. Small or incomplete samples are labelled inconclusive.

## Partnership and commercial scope

**Ruian Offices:** Nominate one location and two merchants, coordinate local access and approvals, select an operating sponsor and agree portfolio-level reporting.

**Participating merchants:** Supply and maintain catalogue/FAQ facts, approved assets and membership terms; authorize eligible data and channels; staff the handoff; approve customer messages and content.

**Decionis:** Lead discovery, adapt Steward, build the scoped services and data adapters, configure approved models, train users and deliver the measurement pack and handover.

The pilot proposal includes configuration and scoped new development. A statement of work will separate implementation fees, hosting/model usage, support and any paid Decionis execution services. No fixed fee, SLA or delivery commitment is quoted before discovery. Hardware, paid media, new loyalty infrastructure, extra languages and additional integrations require separate scope.
